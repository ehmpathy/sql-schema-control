**rule**: .agent/repo=bhuild/role=behaver/briefs/practices/behavior.execution/architect/rule.prefer.decomposable-architecture.md

- src/contract/cli.acceptance.test.ts:18

The execBinRun communicator performs both compute (constructing the shell command via template literal and join) and raw i/o (execSync). ```ts
